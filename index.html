Why it matters

The crisis is real.
The time is now.

Our planet is changing faster than at any point in human history. Here's what's at stake.

Most Critical 🌡️

Climate Change

Global temperatures have risen ~1.1°C since industrialisation. Extreme weather events, sea-level rise, and glacial melt are accelerating at unprecedented rates. The window to limit warming to 1.5°C is closing fast.

02 🌳

Deforestation

10 million hectares of forest disappear every year. Tropical rainforests — home to 80% of terrestrial species — are being cleared for agriculture and industry.

03 🌊

Ocean Pollution

500+ ocean dead zones exist today. Microplastics have infiltrated the food chain and been detected in human blood, breast milk, and lungs.

04 🦋

Biodiversity Loss

Species extinction is happening 100–1000× faster than the natural background rate. A sixth mass extinction is already underway.

05 💨

Air Pollution

99% of the world breathes air that exceeds WHO safety limits. 7 million people die prematurely each year as a result.

06 🏜️

Land Degradation

One-third of Earth's land is degraded. Up to 216 million people could be forced to migrate due to land loss by 2050.

What you can do

Six actions.
Real impact.

You don't have to change everything overnight. Start with one.

01 🚲

Cut Your Carbon

Walk, cycle, or take public transport. Fly less. Switch your energy supplier to renewable sources.

02 ♻️

Ditch Single-Use Plastic

Carry a reusable bag and bottle. Refuse unnecessary packaging. Choose products with less plastic.

03 🥦

Eat More Plants

Reducing red meat — even one day a week — significantly cuts your carbon and water footprint.

04 💡

Save Energy at Home

Switch to LED bulbs. Unplug standby devices. Lower your thermostat by just 1°C to save up to 10% energy.

05 🗂️

Recycle Right

Learn your local rules. Compost food waste. Buy second-hand. Repair before replacing.

06 📢

Use Your Voice

Vote for climate-friendly leaders. Join local green groups. Share verified environmental science.

Daily habits

Small changes.
Big difference.

Simple swaps you can make starting today.

🛒

Shopping

Buy less, buy durable. Choose local, seasonal, and certified-sustainable products.

🍽️

Food

Plan meals to cut waste. Try Meatless Monday. Compost scraps instead of binning them.

🏠

Home

Air-dry laundry. Set AC 2°C warmer. LEDs use 80% less energy than incandescent bulbs.

🚶

Commute

Walk or cycle trips under 5 km. Carpool. Plan routes to avoid unnecessary mileage.

💻

Digital

Video calls over flights — 97% less carbon. Delete unused files and cloud storage regularly.

🌱

Garden

Plant native species. Collect rainwater. Even a balcony pot supports local pollinators.

📚

Stay Informed

Follow IPCC, WWF, UNEP. Verified science leads to informed action and better advocacy.

👨‍👩‍👧

Inspire Others

Lead by example. Connect kids with nature. Culture change starts at home.
